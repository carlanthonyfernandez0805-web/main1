About

Leads international criminal, extradition, and financial crime matters across Europe, with over a decade representing clients before national courts, the CCF, and regulatory bodies.

Sharon Nahari leads the firm’s extradition and INTERPOL practice, coordinating cases across European jurisdictions from first contact through to appeal. Work in this area typically spans European Arrest Warrant proceedings, Red Notice and diffusion challenges before the CCF, and the coordination of local counsel once a matter crosses into a second country’s courts.

Beyond extradition specifically, Sharon Nahari chairs the Israel Bar Association’s Extradition and International Crime Committee, reflecting a broader international criminal law practice that extends well past notice removal and surrender proceedings. Her published commentary has examined how shifting geopolitical conditions and instability across Europe are reshaping cross-border enforcement, as sanctions regimes and international cooperation frameworks respond to conflict and rising tension within the region.

Financial crime is a further core part of the practice. Sharon Nahari’s firm is ranked among Israel’s leading white-collar and criminal law firms, and her own commentary has tracked the shift in financial crime investigation from traditional bank-account analysis toward blockchain and digital-wallet tracing. Matters in this area have included significant money-laundering and fraud allegations against corporate executives and public officials, both domestically and with cross-border dimensions.

Sharon Nahari is regularly instructed at the provisional arrest stage, when the earliest strategic decisions are made and the window for effective action is shortest.
