About

Admitted attorney of the High Court of South Africa, focused on domestic and international criminal law, and cross-border litigation.

Ulrik Olof Hultkrantz is an admitted attorney of the High Court of South Africa, practising as an Associate at C&A Friedlander Attorneys in Cape Town. He holds an LLB from the University of the Western Cape and focuses primarily on domestic criminal law, international criminal law, and general and commercial litigation.

He has experience in court appearances, the drafting of pleadings and affidavits, client consultations, and the management of complex litigation, including high-volume matters involving the protection of minors and inter-jurisdictional extraditions. His criminal practice covers a range of offences and related proceedings.

He is committed to thorough case preparation, strategic advocacy, and the protection of clients’ rights in both domestic and multi-jurisdictional contexts.
